Software solutions

Example builds: sensor prototypes, microcontroller demos, assistive devices, robotics, and physical computing.

Good making starts with good habits

Hackathon guidelines

A few simple rules help keep the event fair, safe, welcoming, and fun for everyone.

  1. Who can join: current Northstar College students from any department may participate. Form a team of 2–4 students; solo registrations will be matched if space allows.
  2. Build during the event: you may arrive with an idea, sketches, and learning resources. The submitted prototype and core implementation must be created during the hackathon.
  3. Credit your sources: open-source libraries and third-party APIs are welcome when permitted. Declare them in your submission and credit all assets, datasets, and collaborators.
  4. Keep it safe: hardware teams must use approved, low-voltage components and follow mentor and lab-safety instructions. No hazardous, destructive, or unauthorized testing.
  5. Respect privacy: do not collect personal, sensitive, or identifiable data without informed permission. Use mock data for demos wherever possible.
  6. Make it welcoming: treat teammates, mentors, volunteers, and other teams with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable.
  7. Be ready to demo: submit your project before the stated deadline, then present a working demo, the problem you addressed, and each team member's contribution.
  8. Judging: judges will consider problem fit, usefulness, working implementation, originality, accessibility, and presentation. Their decision is final.

A few quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a finished idea before I register?

No. A rough direction is enough. You can refine your idea with your team after the event kickoff, as long as your submitted project follows the challenge and event guidelines.

Can students from different departments team up?

Yes. Cross-department teams are welcome, provided every team member is a current Northstar College student.

Can I join without a team?

Yes. Indicate that you are looking for teammates in your registration. Team matching is planned but depends on availability and is not guaranteed.

Will hardware and meals be provided?

Shared tools and some components are planned, but the final inventory is subject to confirmation. Bring your own approved kit if you have one; schedule and meal details will be shared with confirmed teams.

Is this registration form live?

No. This is a sample event website. The form only demonstrates the registration flow in your browser; it does not reserve a place or send your information anywhere.
